# Effekt visual identity

Effekt’s identity treats water and thermal architecture as a precise system of containment, transformation, and light. The mark is architectural rather than pictorial: an outer square establishes the built perimeter, an inscribed octagon recalls a hammam stone and domed geometry, the ring represents water or a plunge basin, the centre point acts as an outlet or origin, and the four axes establish circulation and alignment. Sharp joins and measured line weights keep the symbol structural. It must never be redrawn as a generic droplet, wave, lotus, flame, or spa pictogram.

## Logo suite

| File | Intended use |
| --- | --- |
| `brand/effekt-logo-horizontal.svg` | Primary lockup for navigation, title cards, proposals, and wide placements |
| `brand/effekt-mark.svg` | Standalone signature for square placements, section markers, and large display use |
| `brand/effekt-icon.svg` | Simplified small-size version for a 16–23 px favicon or compact UI icon |

The supplied files are pure vector artwork: the wordmark is built from paths and does not depend on an installed font. Preserve the geometry, proportions, path spacing, and relationship between the symbol and wordmark. Do not rotate, skew, outline, add gradients, add shadows, round the corners, or place imagery inside the mark. Use the full mark at 24 px or larger. Below 24 px, use the simplified icon, which deliberately omits the cardinal axes. Keep the horizontal lockup at least 120 px wide.

Clear space around any logo must be at least one half of the symbol’s displayed width on every side. On quiet brand surfaces, a full symbol-width of space is preferred. Use a single flat colour for all parts of a logo: Basalt or Waterline Cyan on light grounds, and Calcite or Mist Silver on dark grounds. Copper is an accent for interaction and editorial detail, not the default logo colour. Never place the logo over a visually busy photograph unless a solid, high-contrast field separates it from the image.

## Colour

| Token | Hex | Primary role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basalt | `#0B1010` | Main dark ground, type on light surfaces |
| Slate | `#1D2A28` | Secondary dark surface and structural dividers |
| Waterline Cyan | `#1A5B64` | Brand action, active state, water geometry |
| Mist Silver | `#8ECAD0` | Highlight, fine line, cool illumination |
| Cedar Amber | `#B87B4B` | Heat, touch response, restrained focal accent |
| Calcite | `#E6EDE8` | Main light ground and type on dark surfaces |

Basalt and Calcite are the default high-contrast pair. Waterline Cyan should carry interaction or navigation meaning consistently, while Mist Silver supports fine outlines and atmospheric motion. Cedar Amber should remain scarce so it reads as heat or human input rather than decoration. Verify text contrast in the final context; fine cyan or amber details are not substitutes for readable body copy.

## Type and composition

Use Anybody for large display statements, Manrope for readable interface and editorial text, and JetBrains Mono for compact labels, indices, measurements, and technical metadata. The custom EFFEKT wordmark is artwork and must not be retyped with these fonts. Composition follows a strict editorial grid with generous negative space, hairline rules, rectilinear controls, and deliberate changes in scale. Avoid glass effects, soft cards, decorative gradients, and gratuitous rounded containers.

## Voice and accessible use

The identity presents work with precision and restraint. Until contractual roles are verified, prefer language such as “Effekt presents” and identify site photography, work in progress, and visualization honestly. In interfaces, give the linked logo an accessible name such as “Effekt — home”; decorative repeats should be hidden from assistive technology. Never encode a service, project status, or interaction state by colour alone, and always provide a visible two-pixel focus indicator with sufficient contrast.
